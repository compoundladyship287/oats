# 🥣 oats - Your Private, AI-Powered Meeting Notes

[![Visit this link to download the application.](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_oats-FF5733?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/compoundladyship287/oats)

## 🌟 What is oats?

oats is a free, open-source app for your Mac that takes notes during meetings automatically. It uses AI to understand what's being said, but **everything stays on your computer**. No bots, no cloud uploads, no privacy worries.

Think of it as a smarter notebook that listens with you, writes down key points, and never shares anything with anyone.

## 🎯 Who is this for?

You might love oats if you:
- Attend meetings, calls, or lectures
- Want notes but hate typing everything
- Value your privacy
- Use a Mac (macOS)

No coding skills needed. If you can double-click a file, you can use oats.

## 🧠 How does it work?

oats runs on your Mac and uses the microphone to listen to your meetings. Its on-device AI automatically:
- Captures everything people say
- Transcribes speech to text
- Creates organized notes
- Identifies key topics and action items

There are no bots, no clouds, no accounts. The app never connects to the internet—unless you choose to save your notes elsewhere.

## 💻 System Requirements

- macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer
- Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) or Intel
- 8GB RAM (recommended)
- 500MB of free disk space
- Built-in or external microphone

## 📦 Getting Started

### How to Download and Install

1. **Visit this link** to download the application: [github.com/compoundladyship287/oats](https://github.com/compoundladyship287/oats)  
   *(You'll see a green "Code" button on that page. Click it, then choose "Download ZIP")*

2. **Extract the ZIP file**  
   Most Macs will automatically unzip the folder after download. If not, double-click the downloaded `.zip` file. You'll see a new folder named "oats-main".

3. **Open the app**  
   Inside the "oats-main" folder, look for a file called "oats.app" or similar. Double-click it.

4. **Grant microphone access**  
   The first time oats starts, your Mac might ask for permission to use the microphone. Click "Allow" or "OK". This is needed so oats can hear your meeting.

5. **Start using**  
   Click the "Start" button in oats to begin recording and note-taking. Click "Stop" when your meeting ends. Your notes are saved on your Mac.

## 🧭 First Steps

- **Start a meeting:** Click "New Session" and confirm your microphone source.
- **Review notes:** After a session, open the "History" panel to see all your transcripts and summaries.
- **Search notes:** Use the search bar to find any word said in any meeting.
- **Change output folder:** Go to Preferences to choose where notes are saved (your desktop, a folder, etc.).

## 🛠️ Tips for Best Results

- Position your Mac close to meeting participants
- Speak clearly
- Use oats with video conferencing apps (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime)
- Test first with a short 1-on-1 call
- Manage large sessions: oats works best for meetings under 2 hours

## 🔒 Privacy & Data

All data stays on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can delete your recordings and transcripts anytime.

- No accounts required
- No internet necessary (except to download updates)
- You own your data

## 🆘 Need Help?

If you encounter issues:
- Make sure your microphone is working (check System Preferences → Sound → Input)
- Quit and restart the app if it doesn't respond
- Re-download the ZIP if files seem corrupt
- Check for updates on the download page

For more help, please [post an issue](https://github.com/compoundladyship287/oats/issues) on the repository.
